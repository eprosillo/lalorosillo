Lalo Rosillo
Work About

Lalo Rosillo is a photographer based in Austin, Texas. His work focuses on street photography and school sports, and on the moments between the planned ones.

He is bilingual in English and Spanish. He studied Biology and Physics at the University of Texas at El Paso, and his background in event operations, including volunteer leadership at SXSW, shapes how he works: comfortable in busy spaces, paying attention to what is happening around him.
