Work About Contact

Hi, I'm Lalo Rosillo. I'm a photographer based in Austin, Texas, and I make most of my pictures on the street and at school sports events. Every time I press the shutter, I'm trying to notice something real: a gesture, a glance, a moment that happens once and then is gone.

Photography is more than taking pictures for me. It's about making connections and sparking an emotion in whoever is looking. I want my photos to invite you to see an ordinary place or a familiar game in a fresh way.

I came to photography from a different direction. I studied Biology and Physics at the University of Texas at El Paso, and my years in event operations, including volunteer leadership at SXSW, taught me to work calmly in busy, fast-moving spaces. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish, and I bring that same ease to the people I photograph, whichever language they're most comfortable in.

I'm always learning. I keep trying new techniques, pushing my creativity, and growing as a photographer, and each body of work shows where I am in my own evolving style and perspective.

I'm open to collaborations, editorial work, and photography projects. If you have an event, a story, or something creative in mind, I'd love to hear from you and talk about how we might work together. Let's connect.
