Hi, I'm Lalo Rosillo. I'm a photographer based in Austin, Texas. I shoot street in Oaxaca and Guanajuato, architecture in Austin and Boston, and sports at Austin FC and MotoGP. Every time I press the shutter, I'm trying to notice something real: a gesture, a glance, a moment that happens once and then is gone.

Photography is about connection for me. I want my pictures to spark something in whoever is looking, and to make an ordinary place or a familiar game feel new.

I studied Biology at the University of Texas at El Paso, with a minor in Physics. My years in event operations, including volunteer leadership at SXSW, taught me to stay calm in busy, fast-moving spaces. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish, and I bring that same ease to the people I photograph, in whichever language they're most comfortable with.

I'm open to collaborations, editorial work, and photography projects. If you have an event, a story, or something creative in mind, I'd love to hear from you. Let's connect.
